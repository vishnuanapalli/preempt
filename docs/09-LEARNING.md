# Learning log

Two things live here: what was learned building this, and what went wrong along the way.

The second part matters more. A project with no recorded mistakes is either trivial or
dishonest, and "tell me about something that went wrong" is the question that comes up
in every interview. Entries written the day it happened are worth far more than a
reconstruction three months later.

## Post-incident notes

Written whenever review catches something real, a bug reaches the demo, or a
"finished" thing turns out not to be. No blame — the point is the mechanism that let
it through, not the fact that a human missed it.
